# Datasets

## 1. **AI Job Market Trends**

**Description:**  
This dataset gives insights into the global AI/ML job market with over 2,000 realistic listings. It includes details about companies, job roles, skills, salaries, and locations. Ideal for exploring hiring trends, salary predictions, and in-demand skills in the AI field from 2023 to 2025.

**Why I chose it:**  
I want to know what’s happening in the AI job world — who’s hiring, what they’re paying, and which skills are trending before I jump into the race. Gotta know what to master before I master the master’s! 🌍🤖💼

**Source:**  
[https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/abhishekjaiswal4896/ai-job-market-trends/data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/abhishekjaiswal4896/ai-job-market-trends/data)

## 2. **Global Student Migration & Higher Education Trends**

**Description:**  
This dataset explores how students have migrated across the world for higher education between 2019 and 2023. It includes details like countries of origin, study destinations, universities, popular courses, and placement outcomes. Perfect for understanding global study trends, top destinations, and what drives students’ academic journeys.

**Why I chose it:**  
As a student abroad myself, I couldn’t resist seeing where everyone else is heading — maybe I’ll find out if I made the right choice or just followed the crowd! 🌍🎓✈️

**Source:**  
[https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/atharvasoundankar/global-student-migration-and-higher-education-trends/data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/atharvasoundankar/global-student-migration-and-higher-education-trends/data)

## 3. **Climate Change & Agriculture Analysis**

**Description:**  
This dataset explores the impact of climate change on agriculture, including crop yields, weather patterns, and regional farming trends. It helps analyze how changing climates affect food production and which regions or crops are most vulnerable. Perfect for research, predictions, and understanding sustainable farming practices.

**Why I chose it:**  
I’m curious how our planet’s mood swings are messing with our food — plus, I might need to know which crops to avoid if I ever start a garden! 🌱🌎😅

**Source:**  
[https://www.kaggle.com/code/waqi786/climate-change-agriculture-analysis/notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/waqi786/climate-change-agriculture-analysis/notebook)
